- This metric is **sentence/segment recall**, not answer correctness, answer quality or citation support (C's semantic verification and D's comparison quality are separate).
- `hybrid-shared` uses document-type vectors for the question (simulation of a text_type-less server); it is a diagnostic, not the authorized provider configuration.
- The OCR subset (BQ-025/026) is **not** evaluated: local PaddleOCR is unavailable (`pdf_ocr_unavailable`); the provisional expected-text fixture remains gated.
- BQ-021 is an intentional coverage-limited miss (gold off every sampled sweep index) and stays in the denominator; zero-overlap recall is reported at the recorded `sweepCoverage` (see manifest), not as an unbounded claim.
- BQ-016 exposes the single-term lexical proximity artifact (lexical-only miss; hybrid reaches it via sampled index 0).
- Recall ceiling with a perfect embedder is 23/24 (BQ-021 unreachable by design).
- No claim is made about live chat answer quality, latency, or token-cost parity; the controlled variable is the character budget.
